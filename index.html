A face that tells you whether it is looking at you or working.

When it looks at you, the face is written in punctuation: dots, bars and arcs. When it is thinking, talking or lighting your desk, the eyes melt into a single horizon line. This page runs the same rules that drive the robot's screen.

Motion
The rule

Looks at you → C · Works → D

C · Punctuation

Idle, Listen, Nudge. Three strokes only: a dot, a bar and an arc. Friendly and readable at a glance.

D · Horizon

Think, Answer, Sleep, Lamp. One line that ripples, speaks, rests and finally floods the screen with light.

The switch

The eyes melt into the line

Changing role changes form. From C to D the two eyes sink into the horizon; from D to C the line lifts and gathers back into eyes. The change itself tells you what it is doing.
